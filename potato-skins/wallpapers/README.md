**Young stars in Orion** (the NUC's) — Webb Picture of the Month, 2026: a NIRCam field in the Orion molecular cloud, ~1,280 light-years away, catching protostars at every stage of formation, with their jets, outflow cavities and glowing dust.

**Cosmic Cliffs** (the desktop's) — the edge of NGC 3324 in the Carina Nebula, from the James Webb Space Telescope's first full-colour images (July 2022, NIRCam). The "cliffs" are the wall of a giant gas cavity being eroded by ultraviolet light from hot young stars.
