# Wallpapers

**Cosmic Cliffs** — the edge of NGC 3324 in the Carina Nebula, from the James Webb Space Telescope's first full-colour images (July 2022, NIRCam). The "cliffs" are the wall of a giant gas cavity being eroded by ultraviolet light from hot young stars.
